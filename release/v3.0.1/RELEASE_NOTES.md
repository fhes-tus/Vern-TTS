# Vern 3.0.1

This Android update refreshes the offline manual, improves editing imported readings and sharing quotes, and makes it easier to check for updates.

- **Search the offline manual in your own words.** Search for tasks such as “move my books to a new phone” or “audio stops when I lock my phone.” Vern ranks matching chapters and handles common typos. The manual has current screenshots and new guidance for background listening, RSVP Speed Reader, AI Study Studio, extracted-text editing, and accessibility.
- **Find and replace in Edit extracted text.** Move between matches with the previous and next arrows. Replace Next edits the current match, and Replace All updates every match in the current part. Use Undo to reverse an edit before saving.
- **Keep the passage’s formatting in quote cards.** Formatting from the selected passage carries through to the quote preview and shared image.
- **Hear short interruptions without losing your place.** Android can temporarily lower narration volume for short interruptions. When another app takes audio focus, narration pauses.
- **Check for updates through Google Play.** Play installs check Google Play for updates, and About has a direct link to Vern’s Play listing. Standalone installs can still check for releases on GitHub.
