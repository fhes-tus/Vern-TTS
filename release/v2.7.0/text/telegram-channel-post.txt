Vern 2.7.0 · Build 45

This update is about the small things that make reading feel better.

• Scan / OCR now lets you take a photo or choose one from your device.
• Long-press in Listen's Read view and share up to five sentences as a quote image. Pick a colour, preview it, and credit the author or a character.
• PDF Page swipe keeps one page in focus. Swipe left or right to turn it, or switch back to vertical scrolling.
• Reading layout and swipe effects expand inside reader tools. Listen text size now saves separately.
• Files without artwork get proper title-based covers, including the details preview and widgets.
• Study-guide PDF export has moved to Study Hub. Reading lists stay in Library and app Settings.
• Fixed missing contents entries, unsupported-file imports, Funky cards on more screens, awkward greetings and inconsistent dark/AMOLED colours.
• Home's quiet and vibrant colours follow your theme, with the original animated rings retained.
