# Vern 3.0 is here

Vern 3.0 brings major updates to organizing your library, listening to books, studying them, and moving your reading life to a new device. These release notes cover the Android update from 2.8.2 to 3.0.0.

## Major improvements

### Organize your library with Collections

- **Reading Lists are now Collections.** Make a collection for a course, a series, or anything you want to keep together. Create, rename, and delete collections, add or remove files, and move a book between them. They appear in the Library filters and have their own full-screen page in Settings.
- **Listen through a collection in order.** Turn the collection you’ve arranged into a listening queue. Vern shows a confirmation before replacing your current queue.
- **More ways to find a book.** File browsing shows results while it’s still scanning, and you can cancel a scan. Classics can try the University of Waterloo mirror for supported editions when Project Gutenberg is unavailable. *Good Morning, Holy Spirit* and *The Power of Imagination* open Book Explorer with the title ready to search so you can choose a source. Book of the Week stays the same all week and rotates through the curated picks.

### Make a book sound the way you want

- **Give dialogue a cast in Narration Studio.** Vern does a better job of recognizing where narration ends, who’s speaking, and when a speaker is named after a quote. Preview the voices and pauses before you apply them. Your choices carry through to playback and audio export.
- **Teach Vern a pronunciation pattern once.** Set `12/13` to read “12 append 13,” and Pattern can read `15/76` as “15 append 76” while keeping the original numbers. Patterns can also capture words and symbols. Use Text for an exact exception or Regex when you want to write your own rule. Editing a rule keeps its enabled or disabled state and refreshes the reading.
- **More offline voice choices and audio export options.** Alloy, Jessica, Nova, and River join the Studio voice choices. Voice downloads recover more reliably, and Vern points you to Voice Studio when a selected local voice is missing. Export narration as WAV or M4A, see a size estimate, and save the file to your device.
- **More control while listening.** The Reader Timer offers presets and a custom duration, with a choice to pause or stop. It keeps working when you leave the reader screen. In Listen, tap the small three-dot indicator when the spoken line is offscreen to return to it. The collapsed player is slimmer across theme packs, and Glass controls stay visible when a book opens.

### Study from the book you’re already reading

- **The refreshed AI Study Hub is available from a book or the Study overview.** Choose what you want to study, from a passage to a whole reading, and work on flashcards, a practice quiz, a summary, or a study guide. Review the prompt before sending it to an assistant. Longer prompts can travel as a text file. Supported connected assistants can stream responses in Vern, and model choices have been refreshed for people who connect their own API key.
- **Practice the cards you missed.** If you rate a flashcard Again, it can come back during that same session.
- **Read definitions in your language.** Translate the word and its definitions with Google Translate from inside Vern. Browse installed offline dictionary packs by letter, and choose which words and definitions you want to save to your vocabulary.

### Move your library and settings with more control

- **See what’s included before you back up.** Sync & Backup shows the contents before creating a file. Save or share a text-and-settings backup in one step, or choose a portable ZIP when you want to bring media along. The share sheet opens from Backup.
- **Set up weekly backups, review a restore, or share just your settings.** Save a dated library backup to a shared folder for another device to review. Restore previews show what will merge, including readings that need a decision. Use the separate settings file when you only want to transfer your appearance and reader preferences.
- **See what Vern is using on your device.** The full-screen Storage page accounts for books, notes and media, covers, voices, dictionary packs, backups, and cache. Cleanup actions explain what’s selected, and clearing app data requires confirmation.

### A new welcome and easier navigation

- **Start with illustrated setup and First Chapter.** Swipe through the five setup screens, try a voice, and continue in your chosen theme. The original illustrations sit on a consistent light paper background. The optional First Chapter card on Home lets you try importing a reading, setting your listening speed, bookmarking a passage, and saving a note.
- **Move through Vern with less interruption.** Home, Added, Classics, Study, and Notes follow your swipe, with a quick transition on taps. The launch animation carries the icon into the app, and movement throughout the reader and Study respects Reduce motion.

## General app improvements

- **PDF extraction and reading.** Narrow columns, ruled tables, and boxed quotes read in a better order. Wide tables scroll, and runs of stray dots between words are cleaned up. Reading Labels control the page numbers you see while source links keep their PDF anchors. Text and Original do a better job of keeping your place when you switch. Page curls settle more smoothly, and the last line stays anchored when the reader controls hide.
- **Notes and editing.** One link creates one preview, with its thumbnail when available. A subtle highlight shows which formatting tool is active. Full-screen Booknotes separates whole-book and sentence notes into tabs, and sentence excerpts display as plain text. Edit Extracted Text uses familiar rich-text tools, preserves source-page links for unchanged passages, and asks before discarding an unsaved edit.
- **Notes and media recovery.** Saved document notes remain in Study after you remove their source book. Saved Voice Memos are kept with your Notes media and included in portable ZIP backups. Notes keeps your draft available if a save fails.
- **Importing books.** Bulk imports process files in parallel, skip identical copies even when renamed, and preserve the order you chose when they’re added to the queue. The library is more stable when opening books with Glass playback controls.
- **Listening and activity.** Improved handling for more currency formats, Bible references, and punctuation. Reading Insights handles interruptions such as buffering and reader dialogs more consistently. If notifications are off when you set a streak reminder, Vern offers a way to turn them on.
- **Appearance, accessibility, and language.** Deep Navy joins the launcher icon choices with Vern’s white V. More of the reader, Study, Classics, and Reading Insights follows your app language. Narration Studio, Accessibility, Display Theme, and PDF Import Tools each have their own restore-defaults action.
- **After an update.** Vern shows the release notes bundled with the version you installed, even offline. Dismiss them once and they stay dismissed for that version.
